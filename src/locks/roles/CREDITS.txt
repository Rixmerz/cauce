The roles in this folder are locks' own text, written for its one-shot
workers and its result contract. Most of them adapt the agent charters of
vise (https://github.com/Rixmerz/vise, MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Rixmerz):

  answerer          new
  researcher        vise's researcher
  docs-writer       vise's docs-writer
  tester            vise's tester
  refactorer        new, from vise's codelayer skill ("when not to decouple")
  implementer       vise's backend-* charters, made one
  frontend          vise's frontend
  feature-builder   new, with vise's db-migrator on migrations
  debugger          vise's debugger
  investigator      vise's debugger and researcher, and the root-cause-first
                    rule and "three fixes failed: question the architecture"
                    of obra/superpowers' systematic-debugging skill
                    (https://github.com/obra/superpowers, MIT)
  reviewer          vise's reviewer
  security-auditor  vise's security-auditor, carrying Cloudflare's
                    security-audit skill (see ../skills/README.md); "try to
                    disprove each candidate" is that skill's method
  architect         new, from vise's architecture skill; "pick one approach
                    and commit" follows the code-architect agent of
                    anthropics/claude-plugins-official (Apache-2.0)
  frontier          new

The full MIT text of vise is in ../skills/LICENSE-vise.
